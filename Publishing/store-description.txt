Put your vocabulary to the test in Egghead, a relaxed letter game built for thoughtful play and high-score chasing.

Connect neighboring letter tiles to spell words - longer, more ambitious words earn bigger rewards, and the higher a word scores, the better your chance of spawning a valuable bonus tile.

But every move matters - fire tiles appear along the way, and each one burns the tile beneath it every move it isn't used. Use them in a word before the fire reaches the bottom and ends your game.

Take your time, plan ahead, and decide when to play it safe or reach for a spectacular word. Stuck? Shuffle the board for a fresh angle. Your progress is saved after every move, so you can pick up right where you left off.

Features:
- Build words by connecting neighboring letter tiles
- Improve your chance of earning score-multiplying bonus, gold, or diamond tiles by building high-scoring words
- Contain spreading fire tiles before they reach the bottom
- Play at your own pace with no timer
- Create an optional account to save your progress in the cloud and sync across devices
- Beat your personal best, one game at a time

How long can you keep the fire at bay - and how high can you score before it reaches the bottom?
