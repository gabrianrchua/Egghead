**Information Collection and Use**

Egghead stores game progress and preferences on your device. This may include your score, game board, save timestamp, audio and performance preferences, and whether you have chosen local-only mode.

Unless you have chosen local-only mode by explicitly signing out, Egghead uses Unity Authentication to create or restore an anonymous player account and Unity Cloud Save to store your game progress. These services process a Unity Authentication player ID and Internet Protocol ("IP") address. If you choose to add sign-in credentials, Unity Authentication also processes the username and password you provide. These credentials are used to authenticate and access your Egghead account across supported devices. Egghead submits them to Unity Authentication, and CGIXE Productions does not separately store your password.

Cloud-saved game progress may include your score, game board, and save timestamp. This information is associated with your Unity Authentication player ID and is used only to provide authentication, saved-game, account-management, and related service functionality.

Egghead does not use advertising services or in-app purchasing and does not sell your personal information. The app does use third-party services that may collect or process information used to identify you or provide the Service.

**Data Retention and Deletion**

Local game progress and preferences remain on your device until they are deleted through the app, cleared through your device settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to how Android handles app data and backups. Cloud game progress and Unity Authentication account information are retained until the user or CGIXE Productions deletes them, subject to any limited retention required by Unity or Google for security, legal, or service-operational purposes under their own policies.

Deleting saved game data removes the saved game but does not delete the associated player account. You may permanently delete your Egghead player account and its associated local and cloud-saved game data through the in-app **Delete account** option. You may also find instructions for requesting deletion by form or email at [Egghead Account Deletion](https://sites.google.com/view/cgixe-productions/egghead-account-deletion), or contact [CGIXEProductions+egghead@gmail.com](mailto:CGIXEProductions+egghead@gmail.com).
